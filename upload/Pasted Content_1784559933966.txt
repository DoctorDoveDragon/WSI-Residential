## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Well Spring Intervention LLC has established a foundational SOP manual that addresses many operational and clinical requirements for a Level 3 Supervised Residential Group Home. The manual demonstrates awareness of key regulatory frameworks including 10A NCAC 27G, NC Medicaid CCP 8C, and Rule 108 (10A NCAC 27T). However, **substantial gaps exist** between the current SOP and the mandatory requirements of the RMDM (Effective July 8, 2025). These gaps pose significant compliance risk, potential for audit findings, and vulnerability to recoupment of Medicaid funds.

**Overall Risk Rating: HIGH** – Multiple critical requirements are unaddressed or inadequately documented.

## SECTION 1: RECORDS ADMINISTRATION AND REPORTING REQUIREMENTS (RMDM Chapter 1)

### 1.1 Record Retention and Disposition

| RMDM Requirement | SOP Provision | Finding |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Clinical service records of minors must be retained for **12 years after the minor has reached age 18** (until age 30)【8†L3】 | "Records retained for a minimum of **10 years** from date of discharge" (Section 10.3) | **NON-COMPLIANT.** The SOP's 10-year retention period is shorter than the RMDM's 12-year post-majority requirement. This is a critical deficiency. |
| Clinical service records of adults retained **11 years after last encounter**【8†L3】 | Not separately addressed | **GAP.** No distinction made between adult and minor retention periods. |
| Destruction of records not listed in a schedule requires authorization from DMH/DD/SUS and Division of Archives and Records | Not addressed | **GAP.** |
| Record abandonment is a violation of state/federal law subject to sanctions【8†L6】 | Not addressed | **GAP.** No policy on record abandonment. |

**AUDITOR RECOMMENDATION:** Revise Section 10.3 to explicitly state: *"Clinical records for minors shall be retained for 12 years after the individual reaches age 18 (i.e., until age 30). Clinical records for adults shall be retained for 11 years after the last encounter. The most stringent applicable requirement shall prevail."* Add policies on record abandonment and destruction authorization.

## CONCLUSION

Well Spring Intervention LLC has established a foundational operational framework, but **the current SOP Version 1.0 is not fully compliant with the RMDM (Effective July 8, 2025)** . The identified gaps—particularly in record retention, CDW reporting, accounting of disclosures, CCA requirements, service orders, and service note content—represent significant compliance risk. Without corrective action, the provider is vulnerable to:

- Audit findings and potential recoupment of Medicaid funds
