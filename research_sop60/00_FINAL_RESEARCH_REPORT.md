## 2. MINIMUM STAFFING RATIOS — Codified Text of 10A NCAC 27G .1704

### Authoritative Codified Text (Cornell LII)

**Rule .1704 — MINIMUM STAFFING REQUIREMENTS**

**(a)** A qualified professional shall be available by telephone or page. A direct care staff shall be able to reach the facility within 30 minutes at all times.

**(b) WAKING HOURS** — minimum direct care staff when children/adolescents are present and awake:
- **(b)(1)** 1-4 children/adolescents: **TWO** direct care staff shall be present
- **(b)(2)** 5-8 children/adolescents: **THREE** direct care staff shall be present
- **(b)(3)** 9-12 children/adolescents: **FOUR** direct care staff shall be present

**(c) SLEEP HOURS** — minimum direct care staff during child/adolescent sleep hours:
- **(c)(1)** 1-4 children/adolescents: **TWO** direct care staff present, **ONE** shall be AWAKE (other may sleep)
- **(c)(2)** 5-8 children/adolescents: **TWO** direct care staff present, **BOTH** shall be AWAKE
- **(c)(3)** 9-12 children/adolescents: **THREE** direct care staff present, **TWO** shall be AWAKE (third may sleep)

**(d)** Additional direct care staff required based on individual needs as specified in treatment plan.
**(e)** Facility shall ensure supervision of children/adolescents when away from facility.

### Assessment of Document Discrepancies

#### SOP Manual v2.24 Claim: "2:4 minimum (2 awake staff per 1-4 youth 24/7) per .1704"
- **WAKING:** Codified minimum is 2 staff PRESENT for 1-4 youth. The "2 awake" characterization is implicit (during waking hours, both staff are awake by definition). SOP Manual's claim is essentially consistent with .1704(b)(1).
- **SLEEPING:** Codified minimum is 2 staff present, **1 must be AWAKE** (the other MAY sleep). The SOP Manual's claim of "2 awake staff per 1-4 youth 24/7" is MORE RESTRICTIVE than the codified minimum. This is permissible as a programmatic standard but constitutes a citation error if presented as "the minimum."
- **Verdict:** SOP Manual OVERSTAFFS (acceptable best practice), but citation error. **Recommend correction to:** "Per 10A NCAC 27G .1704(c)(1), 2 direct care staff shall be present during sleep hours for 1-4 youth, with at least 1 awake. Well Spring elects to staff 2 awake staff as a best-practice standard above the minimum."

#### CARF Plans v1.2 Claim: "1:6 waking / 1:8 overnight"
- **WAKING:** For 6 youth, the rule requires **3 staff** (1:2 ratio) for 5-8 youth under .1704(b)(2). The CARF Plans' "1:6" implies 1 staff for 6 youth — significantly below codified minimum.
- **OVERNIGHT:** For 8 youth, the rule requires **2 staff BOTH AWAKE** (1:4 ratio) for 5-8 youth under .1704(c)(2). The CARF Plans' "1:8" implies 1 staff for 8 youth — significantly below codified minimum.
- **Verdict:** CARF Plans v1.2 is **NON-COMPLIANT** — below codified minimum. The "1:8" ratio appears to have been incorrectly imported from the .1200 series (.1202 — psychosocial rehabilitation adult day/night facility staff ratio of "one staff member on-site to each eight or fewer clients"). The .1200 series is inapplicable to a .1700 staff-secure children's facility. **Mandatory correction of CARF Plans v1.2 required.**

### Related Staffing Requirements (within .1700 series)
- **.1702:** QP hours — minimum 10 hrs/week for 5 or fewer beds; 32 hrs/week for 6+ beds. 70% during awake/present hours.
- **.1703:** At least one full-time associate professional.
- **.1705:** At least 4 hours/week face-to-face clinical consultation by a licensed professional.
- **.1704(a):** QP available by phone/page; direct care staff can reach facility within 30 minutes at all times.

## 3. CAPACITY LIMIT

### Authoritative Codified Text

**Rule .1706(a):** "Each facility shall serve no more than a total of **12 children and adolescents**."

### Assessment
- **SOP Manual v2.24 Claim:** "Max 9 children per state group-home definition" — **INCORRECT.**
- **Investigation of the "9" figure:** The number 9 appears in NC DHSR Glossary for "Group home for developmentally disabled adults" (2 to 9 disabled adult residents — an ADULT CARE HOME definition under NCAC 10A 13, inapplicable to children's behavioral health facilities). No NC rule caps children's staff-secure RTF capacity at 9.
- **Zoning threshold:** NC Gen Stat 160D-907 caps "family care home" zoning protection at 6 or fewer residents — NOT 9.
- **General capacity rule (.0404):** "A facility shall accept no more clients than the number for which it is licensed" (license-specific capacity).
- **Verdict:** SOP Manual claim of "Max 9" is a citation error. The actual maximum for a .1700 staff-secure children's facility is **12 per .1706(a)**.
- **Recommended correction:** Replace "Max 9 children per state group-home definition" with "Max 12 children per 10A NCAC 27G .1706(a) for Residential Treatment Staff Secure for Children or Adolescents."

### Building Code Implications
- **6 or fewer residents** → Group R-3 (single-family residential classification); qualifies as "family care home" for zoning.
- **7-12 residents** → Group R-4 (Residential Care/Assisted Living) per NC Building Code §425/§428; NOT a "family care home" for zoning — Wake County UDO may require Special/Conditional Use Permit under NCGS 160D-705.
- **13+ residents** → NOT permissible under .1700 staff-secure children's facility cap.
